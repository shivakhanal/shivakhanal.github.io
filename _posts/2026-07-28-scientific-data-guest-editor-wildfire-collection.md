I'm co-Guest Editor, alongside [Florent Mouillot](https://www.nature.com/collections/dgjagdcbii/guest-editors) (IRD, France), of a new *Scientific Data* Collection, [Data for wildfire monitoring, prevention and management](https://www.nature.com/collections/dgjagdcbii/guest-editors).

The collection welcomes datasets on active fire and burned area monitoring (satellite, UAV, or other remote-sensing tools), ecological studies of wildfire-affected ecosystems, and related hydrological, meteorological, policy, and risk-assessment work. Submissions are open now, with a deadline of 27 January 2027.

More details and submission guidelines are on the [collection page](https://www.nature.com/collections/dgjagdcbii/guest-editors).
