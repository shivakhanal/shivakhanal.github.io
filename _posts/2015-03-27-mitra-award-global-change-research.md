In 2015 I received the **Mitra Award for Global Change Research** from the [Asia-Pacific Network for Global Change Research (APN)](https://www.apn-gcr.org/), while working at the Department of Forest Research and Survey, Kathmandu, Nepal. The award was presented in conjunction with the 20th APN Intergovernmental Meeting and Scientific Planning Group (IGM/SPG) Meeting, hosted by the Government of Nepal in Kathmandu from 25 to 27 March 2015, where I presented my work on [*Integration of object-based image analysis with machine learning algorithm for forest type classification in Nepal*](https://www.apn-gcr.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/20IGM-NS06-Khanal.pdf).

## About the award

The Mitra Award was established by APN in 2010 in memory of Dr. Ashesh Proshad Mitra, who served as APN's Scientific Planning Group Member for India from 1996 to 2007. It recognises outstanding young scientists conducting global change research in the Asia-Pacific region, and is presented in conjunction with the annual APN IGM/SPG Meetings.

## Looking back

Writing for the APN annual report at the time, I noted that "APN provided a great opportunity for me to present my research studies to global change researchers" and that the award had "further motivated me to continue research activities in the field of global environment change." A few months later I was selected for the [GOFC-GOLD Data Initiative Advanced Training](/activities/gofc-gold-data-initiative-training/) organised by START, and I have since continued to work with APN as a [member of its Capacity Development Committee](https://www.apn-gcr.org/person/shiva-khanal/).
