Uses over 20 years of MODIS FPAR time series (2001-2022), combined with S-mode PCA and k-means clustering, to classify forest phenology types across New South Wales eucalypt forests and quantify canopy recovery speed after fire. Compares prescribed burns against wildfires across severity classes. Median landscape-wide recovery time was under a year, but trajectories diverged by fire type: prescribed burns recovered notably faster within the first two years, while the long tail of slow-recovering areas beyond two years was similar between fire types.

Collaboration with Amila Wickramasinghe, Ivan Kotzur, and Matthias Boer at the Hawkesbury Institute for the Environment, Western Sydney University.
