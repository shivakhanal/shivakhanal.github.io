Models plot-based estimates of forest soil organic carbon (SOC) using covariates related to climate, soil, and topographic position, producing Nepal's first high-resolution, quantile random forest map of national forest SOC stocks together with prediction uncertainties. Estimates 494 million tonnes (SE = 16) of topsoil (0–30 cm) SOC in Nepal's forests, and shows that high-elevation forests in the Central Himalayas hold disproportionately large SOC stocks that are significantly underrepresented in global-scale carbon assessments.

Collaboration with Rachael H. Nolan, Belinda E. Medlyn, and Matthias M. Boer at the Hawkesbury Institute for the Environment, Western Sydney University.
