Analyzes land use and land cover change (2000-2020) in the Annapurna Conservation Area, showing forest recovery at mid-elevations alongside continued glacier retreat and settlement expansion. Projects continued forest and settlement gains, and snow/glacier decline, through 2050 under SSP245 and SSP585 scenarios.
