Draws on field observations from Nepal's national forest inventory (2010–2014) to present the most comprehensive georeferenced, plot-level dataset to date of forest carbon stocks in the country: aboveground biomass (AGB) estimates from 2,009 inventory plots and soil organic carbon (SOC) estimates from 1,156 plots. Fills a longstanding knowledge gap on forest carbon in the Central Himalayas, a region of highly heterogeneous terrain and rich biodiversity that remains poorly represented in existing global forest carbon estimates.

Collaboration with Matthias M. Boer at the Hawkesbury Institute for the Environment, Western Sydney University.
