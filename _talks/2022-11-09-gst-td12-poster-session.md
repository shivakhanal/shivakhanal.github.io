Presented a poster summarizing my submission to the first [Global Stocktake (GST)](https://unfccc.int/topics/global-stocktake/components-of-the-gst/technical-dialogues-of-the-first-global-stocktake/technical-dialogue-11-td11-of-the-first-global-stocktake) under the Paris Agreement, as part of the [TD1.2 poster session](https://unfccc.int/global-stocktake-td12-poster-session), held in front of Plenary II - Ramses.

The [Technical Dialogues](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/sb2023_09E.pdf) of the first GST were a series of inclusive, participatory discussions held between 2022 and 2023, co-facilitated by UNFCCC appointees, engaging governments, experts, and stakeholders to evaluate collective climate progress and guide future ambition. The co-facilitators invited everyone who had made a submission to the GST to present a poster summarizing it, displayed at COP27 to make submissions more visual and accessible to all participants.
