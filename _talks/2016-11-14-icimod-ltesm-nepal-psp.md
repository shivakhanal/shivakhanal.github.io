Presented Nepal's country case study, [Monitoring forest resources of Nepal using permanent sample plots established for forest resource assessment](https://lib.icimod.org/records/4r76n-96j35) (Annex 10), at the [Regional Orientation on Long-Term Environmental and Socio-Ecological Monitoring](https://www.icimod.org/event/regional-orientation-on-long-term-environmental-and-socio-ecological-monitoring/), a five-day workshop (14–18 November 2016) organized by ICIMOD's Kangchenjunga Landscape Conservation and Development Initiative and the Institute of Forestry, Tribhuvan University, with support from GIZ and ADA. The workshop brought together 48 participants from four transboundary landscape initiatives across Bhutan, China, India, and Nepal to build capacity for long-term ecological monitoring and to draft country-level action plans.

The talk introduced Nepal's national network of permanent sample plots (PSPs), re-measured periodically to track forest growth, stocking, and change, as the country's core long-term data source for forest resource assessment, and situated it within the workshop's broader push toward standardized, comparable monitoring protocols across the Hindu Kush Himalaya.
