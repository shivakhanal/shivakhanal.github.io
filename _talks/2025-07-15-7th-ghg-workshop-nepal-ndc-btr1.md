Presented at the [7th GHG Inventory System Training Workshop](https://unosd.un.org/en/events/7thGHGWorkshop), co-organized by UNOSD, UNESCAP, UNFCCC, the IPCC, and CBIT-GSP, and hosted by the Government of Kazakhstan.

The talk covered Nepal's national circumstances and institutional arrangements for greenhouse gas (GHG) inventory preparation, key GHG emission trends by sector (Energy, IPPU, Agriculture, LULUCF, and Waste) from 1994 to 2022, and Nepal's mitigation ambition under NDC 3.0 for 2030 and 2035. It also outlined challenges faced in GHG inventory compilation, adaptation planning, and NDC 3.0 preparation, along with support received through the BTR Preparation Project and CBIT project.
