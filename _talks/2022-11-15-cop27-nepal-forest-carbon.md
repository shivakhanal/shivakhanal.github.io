Presented findings from my PhD thesis, *'Quantification of Nepal's forest carbon stocks'*, at COP27 as a member of the Nepalese Government delegation, where I also led a section on measuring greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. The work assessed forest carbon storage in the Central Himalayas, a region with exceptional climatic and vegetation variation over short distances, providing nation-wide, high-resolution estimates of Nepal's forest carbon stocks to support compliance with UN REDD+ monitoring and reporting requirements.

Covered by Western Sydney University's Hawkesbury Institute for the Environment: [Western PhD candidate presents new research at 2022 United Nations Climate Change Conference, helping quantify forest carbon stocks in Nepal](https://www.westernsydney.edu.au/news-centre/stories/2022/western-phd-candidate-presents-new-research-at-2022-united-nations-climate-change-conference-helping-quantify-forest-carbon-stocks-in-nepal)
