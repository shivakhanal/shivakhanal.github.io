This presentation gives a brief overview of the early experiences with the ongoing Forest Resource Assessment of Nepal. It explains the methodology applied and the variables measured, and gives details on the completion of the field inventory in the Terai, a physiographic region in the south. It further highlights the plans and possible challenges for the remaining area of the country, the hills and mountains.

Delivered in Session 1 (Large-scale forest inventory setups and related modeling procedures) of the research seminar at FIPI, part of the 1st workshop of the ICI project *Improving Research Capacity of Forest Resource Information Technology in Vietnam and Nepal*, organised by Metla (Finnish Forest Research Institute).
