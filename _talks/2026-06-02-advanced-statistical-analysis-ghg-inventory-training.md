Served as trainer for a four-day training on advanced statistical data analysis for Nepal's Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Inventory, held from 2 to 5 June 2026 at Godawari Village Resort, Lalitpur. Organised with Capacity Building Initiaties in Transparency (CBIT) project.

The course covered data management, basic statistical analysis in R and understanding model relationships, sensitivity analysis techniques, an introduction to uncertainty analysis, handling missing data, and an introduction to report writing with Markdown.
